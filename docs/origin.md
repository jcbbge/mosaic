# Spatial information lab

Exploration record, not an implementation brief. Preserve hypotheses as hypotheses.

## Domain-agnostic purpose

Explore spatial navigation, information density and viewer-dependent interpretation
as a domain-agnostic interface approach. It must not assume software development,
programming, a particular field of study, industry or organizational structure.
Made Well is a convenient example, not the organizing model or the ultimate scope.

The operator names academic research, sales teams and medical practice as other
possible applications. These are examples to test the approach against, not a
request to build domain products or specialized integrations now.

Separate the candidate general mechanism (space, focus, representation and reader
profile) from domain-specific meaning (what a region contains, how its information
is related, and which interpretations are appropriate). Do not bake discovery,
delivery, jobs, calls, telemetry or developer seniority into the general model.
Nor should the general approach presume that every subject is a workflow, has
live activity, ends in logs, or fits a fixed hierarchy. These are questions for
research rather than constraints to impose on every domain.

## Frozen control

`lab/three-lens-experiments` preserves the shape-only prototype in
`lab/motion/index.html`. Further exploration starts on `lab/information-display`.
The control has four motion studies and no product data. Its two corner marks are
currently drawings, not working knobs. No branch has been pushed for this lab.

The operator prefers pivot and shutter: they feel mechanical and tactile.
Scale/fade and hybrid introduce disorientation through changing opacity. Preserve
those studies for comparison; do not treat opacity-based motion as the preferred path.

## Two independent controls

### Left: spatial navigation

The lower-left mechanical affordance operates the spatial mechanism: turn a dial,
crank, or equivalent to pivot or move the shutter. Its exact interaction is open.
This changes where the viewer is looking and which region becomes primary.
The left half is the primary legible workspace; peripheral regions communicate
information at representations appropriate to their available space.

The initially pinned lower-left pivot is one candidate, not a required architecture.
Rotation, coordinated sliding planes, and other mechanisms remain explorable.

### Right: interpretation profile

The lower-right mechanical affordance changes how the same underlying information
is read or presented for a viewer. This is not merely a color, typography or layout
theme, and it is not another depth/navigation control.

Software-specific examples from the operator, illustrative rather than defaults
for the general system:

- A high-level, nontechnical interpretation by default, with other profiles for
  more technical, intermediate or senior developers.
- For a code tutorial, the same underlying concept expressed through React, Vue
  or SolidJS examples. These are illustrative alternative profile sets, not a
  single prescribed sequence mixing frameworks and expertise levels.

The underlying facts or concept stay the same. Vocabulary, explanation, examples,
notation and emphasis may differ; the profile must not fabricate facts or alter
the work's state. Framework examples express the same concept, not identical code.

The two controls must compose:

- Changing the right profile preserves the selected region, spatial position and
  navigation history; it does not rotate or relocate the viewer.
- Spatial navigation preserves the chosen interpretation profile.
- At any location, a viewer can examine that same subject through another profile.

These controls are conceptual requirements to explore, not implemented behavior.
Made Well's UX/DX/AX lenses are one possible domain-specific interpretation-profile
set, not a universal audience taxonomy or a settled assignment of profiles to
spatial panes or levels of developer seniority.

## Spatial structure and information

- The seven visible panes are the operator's current candidate maximum density,
  not seven established semantic categories or a mandatory hierarchy depth.
- Navigation exposes fewer remaining panes near the terminal full-frame view.
  Bounded overview/core endpoints may help orientation; their geometry is open.
- For Made Well, an overview could expose discovery and delivery as two major
  zones. Traversal toward the core could ultimately reveal raw event telemetry.
  This is a domain example, not the required structure or endpoint of the general
  interface. Other subjects may lead toward source material, observations or
  other forms of detail rather than an event log.
- An eighth pane is an open possibility for a useful mapping, not a decision made
  solely to obtain symmetry or powers of two.
- Distinguish subject/scope, size-based representation and interpretation profile.
  A smaller pane does not inherently mean a deeper subject or a less technical reader.
- Peripheral representations may use graphics, marks, patterns or shades rather
  than miniature text-heavy interfaces. They must communicate actual information.
- Where a domain has activity, accepted progress, errors or pauses, these are
  different signals. Heat-like intensity is a candidate encoding, not proof of
  progress or health. Static information must also be considered.
- Direct selection of a zone should be explored: promote it to the primary
  workspace without forcing the viewer through every intervening stop. Preserve
  an understandable return path and the selected interpretation profile.

## Independent research directions

Not launched yet. Each should return candidate approaches, failure modes and one
small experiment, not a finished design or changes to the frozen control.
Start with domain-agnostic principles and challenge them with contrasting domain
examples. Report which findings generalize and which require domain-specific
meaning; do not turn this into five software-interface investigations.

1. **Information structure:** subjects, relationships, grouping, aggregation and
   overview-to-detail navigation. Explore where a hierarchy fits and where it
   does not; discovery/delivery is only one example.
2. **Representation and profiles:** size-based semantic zoom versus reader-based
   interpretation; how the two compose without changing identity or truth.
3. **Peripheral visual language:** meaningful nontextual encodings for both static
   and changing information, with domain-appropriate meanings and without relying
   on color alone. Activity, progress, pause and failure are examples, not the
   universal vocabulary.
4. **Navigation and orientation:** direct jumps, return paths, bounded endpoints,
   left-hand mechanism affordance and an independent right-hand profile control.
5. **Legibility during motion:** stationary versus moving information, when detail
   appears, and differences between pivot and shutter without disorienting fades.

## Six application studies — 2026-10-26

The operator supplied subjects for six working prototypes, now in
`lab/applications/`. The original `lab/motion/index.html` remains the frozen
shape-only control. These studies are not Made Well features or a decision to
build six products. They have no live inference or external service integration.

| Study | Supplied material | Working encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perspective instrument | Why model weights and provider experiences differ | Three authored explanations of each concept; source links and an observation record |
| Branching playground | A cohesive company brain without another writing destination or service proliferation | Open finding/handoff possibilities, create a personal branch, and return to the saved parent region |
| Unfolding encounter | Capricorn, INTJ, inherited wisdom, daily/lunar/seasonal rhythms | Separate personal resonance, evidence and observations; keep observation and interpretation apart |
| Conversation observatory | This conversation | Selected source excerpts, changing interpretations, unresolved questions and additive corrections |
| Composition workbench | Agent memory beyond collecting artifacts | Collect lens-specific fragments, reorder, explicitly assemble, edit and export with origins retained |
| Competing explanations | Gaming after 30 | Hold concern and defense apart; vary an invented situation without calculating a moral or clinical verdict |

All six expose pivot, shutter, scale/fade and hybrid as options. The left control
changes the focused region; the right changes the authored reading without
changing region, branch or personal composition. Peripheral regions use titles
when they fit and numbered marks when they do not. Direct jumps remain possible;
they are not an obligation to traverse every intervening stop. Text is hidden
during geometric motion and returns upright at rest; reduced-motion navigation
settles immediately. The pink terminal region grows to the full frame.

The geometry is a presentation sequence, not a claim that every subject is a
seven-level hierarchy. The branching study additionally opens independent local
sequences. A branch is a possibility, never a commitment or executable action.
Interpretation means a different expression of the same subject, not identical
information: compression can omit detail, and a challenge can expose uncertainty.

Inputs and navigation are saved in this browser's local storage; JSON export
retains the selected study's material and inputs. No cross-device synchronization
is promised. Storage failures are visible. Clearing inputs requires confirmation
and affects only the selected study; replacing an edited draft also requires
confirmation. Invented company handoffs and gaming scenarios are labeled. The
conversation map is selected excerpts, not a complete transcript. Sources for
learning/evidence claims are linked in the affected regions; human-memory
analogies do not establish agent sentience or equivalent cognitive mechanisms.

### Decision and acceptance

Reuse the standalone study's geometry rather than coupling the experiment to the
engine or adding a framework. **DX hit** (high leverage / low adoption friction):
one small shell supports all studies, at the cost of hand-authored content and no
live AI. **UX hit** (expected high delight / medium cognitive load): recognizable
controls, bounded motion and recoverable branches; the unfamiliar layout remains
an experiment rather than a claimed usability improvement. **AX hit** (high
verification capability / low implementation ambiguity): deterministic authored
states, standard DOM controls, inspectable sources and export; no inference
capability is added. Three hits justify the local studies, not a product rollout.
No Made Well operating rule changes, so the application foundation is unchanged.

Acceptance: inspect all six populated studies; exercise four motion modes and
three readings; verify the left-half and full-frame endpoints, direct selection,
branch return, independent lens state, persistence, source preservation,
composition ordering/draft protection, scenario conditions, export and a narrow
layout. Browser checks cover the lab; `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` guard the
existing application. User testing of comprehension, discovery and disorientation
is still outstanding. Do not equate render correctness with those outcomes.
